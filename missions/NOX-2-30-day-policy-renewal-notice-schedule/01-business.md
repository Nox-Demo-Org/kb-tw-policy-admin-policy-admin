# Business requirement: 30-Day Policy Renewal Notice Schedule

## The request
"Change the renewal notice schedule so policyholders receive their notices 30 days before policy expiration rather than 21 days."

## Problem
Currently, policyholders receive their renewal notices only 21 days before their policy expires. This 21-day timeline was put in place years ago to accommodate an old physical print and mail vendor contract that ended in 2024.

With only three weeks to review their updated premium, assess their cover options, and complete their renewal, many customers feel rushed. Some do not see the notice in time or leave renewing to the last minute, leading to lapsed policies. Our current renewal conversion sits at 73%. Giving customers more breathing room is projected to raise renewal conversion to 82%, recovering valuable customer retention and premium income.

## Who is affected
- **Policyholders**: All existing Tidewell Mutual home and motor insurance customers approaching their annual policy expiration.
- **Customer Support and Renewals Team**: Operations and support staff handling renewal enquiries, policy questions, and retention calls, who will see renewal conversations spread over a more manageable window.

## What should change
- Policyholders will receive their renewal notice, updated pricing, and policy schedule 30 days before their policy expiration date instead of 21 days before.
- Renewal communications, customer portal reminders, and payment invitations will begin generating and sending at the 30-day mark.
- Customers will have an extra 9 days to review their coverage, contact customer support if needed, and accept their renewal terms.

## What "done" looks like
- The business can verify in standard daily policy activity reports and customer communications logs that renewal notices are dispatched exactly 30 days prior to policy expiration dates.
- Renewal conversion metrics on the monthly operations dashboard begin tracking toward the target 82% conversion rate.

## Examples

### Example 1: Home insurance renewal
- **Policyholder**: Sarah Jenkins, Home Buildings and Contents policy expiring on 15 November 2026.
- **Before**: Sarah's renewal notice is sent on 25 October 2026 (21 days prior). Sarah is on holiday for a week and has only 14 days upon return to review her renewal quote before coverage lapses.
- **After**: Sarah's renewal notice is sent on 16 October 2026 (30 days prior). She receives it before leaving, reviews her coverage, and confirms her renewal with plenty of time.

### Example 2: Motor insurance renewal with cover questions
- **Policyholder**: Marcus Davies, Comprehensive Motor policy expiring on 1 July 2026.
- **Before**: Marcus receives his renewal notice on 10 June 2026 (21 days prior). He wants to adjust his voluntary excess and add a named driver, but due to the tight deadline and support queue times, his policy temporarily lapses before changes are finalised.
- **After**: Marcus receives his renewal notice on 1 June 2026 (30 days prior). He has ample time to discuss his policy options with customer support and renew seamlessly before 1 July.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Renewal notices for upcoming expiring policies are dispatched to policyholders 30 days before their expiration date instead of 21 days.
- [ ] Policyholders who log into the customer portal see their renewal quote and renewal options available starting 30 days prior to policy expiration.
- [ ] Customer service staff can confirm in the customer history log that renewal communications were triggered 30 days ahead of the policy end date.
- [ ] Change the renewal notice schedule so policyholders receive their notices 30 days before policy expiration rather than 21 days.
